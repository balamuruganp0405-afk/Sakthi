Profile Summary

Professional Journey

I am Sakthi Shanmugam, a Chartered Engineer (India) with over 10 years of professional experience in mechanical engineering, engineering design, customs valuations, damage assessment, and technical report drafting.

My academic foundation stems from a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, subsequently supplemented by a Master's degree in Engineering Design. I hold memberships across several nationally recognized institutions, ensuring that every engineering review, inspection, and valuation report stands up to rigorous legal and technical guidelines.

Educational Mission

I believe in bridging the gap between academic theory and practical engineering compliance. Through this study portal, I aim to share field-tested knowledge, clarify engineering doubts, and mentor aspiring Chartered Engineers in India to prepare precise, compliant technical dossiers.
