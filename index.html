FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Chartered Engineer certifications, exam details, or study checklists? Find clear answers below.

A Chartered Engineer (India) is a certified professional recognized under the royal charter of the Institution of Engineers (India). They verify technical reports, assess equipment values, and certify machinery safety and residual life for export, import, government, and legal processes.

To obtain the Chartered Engineer (CEng) certification, you must first become a corporate member (AMIE, MIE, or FIE) of the Institution of Engineers (India) (IEI). This requires an accredited engineering degree, relevant professional experience, and the submission of detailed technical work summaries.
