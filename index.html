FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about Chartered Engineer certifications, machinery valuations, or customs inspections? Find clear answers below.

A Chartered Engineer (India) is a certified professional recognized under the royal charter of the Institution of Engineers (India). They are authorized to inspect machinery, issue compliance certificates, assess equipment value, and verify technical reports for export, import, government, and legal processes.

Under India's Foreign Trade Policy, all second-hand or used capital goods imported into the country must be accompanied by a Chartered Engineer Certificate (CEC). This certificate verifies the equipment's residual life, year of manufacture, current market value, and compliance with national safety and environmental standards.

A typical on-site visual inspection takes 1 to 2 hours per machine, depending on its size and complexity. Following the inspection, the final verified valuation report and Chartered Engineer certificate are compiled and delivered within 24 to 48 hours.

Yes, all certifications and valuation reports are drafted in strict accordance with the guidelines set by the Director General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) and Indian Customs authorities, ensuring hassle-free custom clearance coordinates and compliance.
